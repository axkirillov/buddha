| Legend | Suttas |
| --------| ------- |
| Gotama was a prince, his father Suddhodana was a king | Gotama was from a ruling class family, but he was not royalty[1]. |
| After Gotama's birth, ascetic Asita predicted, that he would become either a great spiritual teacher or a great political leader. | Asita saw that the boy *shall reach the highest awakening*. The "either ascetic or king" prediction is not found in the Suttas[2].|
| To prevent Gotama from knowing anything of the ugly realities of life and thereby becoming a renunciant sage, as Asita had predicted, his father had him confined in a beautiful palace, provided with every means of sensual gratification | Another story not found in the Cannon. Gotama describes himself having 3 residences for each season [3]. According to Ven. S. Dhammika the word Pāsāda could also be translated as mansion, villa or manor house [1].|
| The story of the four signs. Having escaped the palace, Gotama sees an old man, a sick man, a corpse and an ascetic. This convinces Gotama that he has to become an ascetic himself. | This seems to have been borrowed from the canonical story of another Buddha - Vipassī[4]. Buddha Gotama, on the other hand, leaves home after some deep reflection [5].|
